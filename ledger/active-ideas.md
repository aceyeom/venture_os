### PARK-5 · De-Smell / Refresh Booth (AXIS-G inaugural, cycle 30) — *honest non-kill*
**One-sentence (AXIS-G, MODE-CASH):** people leaving 고깃집/이자카야/clubs (and pre-date/pre-interview) want to lose food/smoke/sweat smell fast; today they carry Febreze or own a 스타일러; we'd place a per-use **refresh booth** (≈60-90s heated ionized-air deodorize + light fragrance finish) in nightlife/restaurant/airport zones, monetized per-use (QR/coin) + venue rev-share + fragrance-brand sampling sponsorship.
**Why PARK, not KILL (the calibration case):** the FUNCTION is owned — LG 스타일러 + 삼성 에어드레서 (~90% of the 의류관리기 market, ₩2M units, market 3.8× in 4 yrs) do "de-smell your clothes" at home, and a ₩5k Febreze-class spray does ~80% of it (PHYS-5 / L2 cheap-substitute). BUT the FORMAT — a placed, paid, at-the-moment-of-need person-refresh booth — is genuine whitespace in KR (smoking/industrial deodorizing booths exist; a consumer refresh booth doesn't), and 인생네컷/포토이즘 prove the placed-booth per-use model transacts (₩80-100M to open, real monthly revenue). So a real but UNVALIDATED angle survives → PARK, per the v2.9 calibration rule.
**Gate notes:** PHYS-2 the device is cloneable (L8/L14) → moat must be venue-exclusive placement + brand, like 포토이즘 holds locations. PHYS-3 capital/ops moderate (machine + refills + servicing) — a 1-2 unit pilot fits 20-25 hr/wk; scaling needs fund/hire. PHYS-4 fragrance = 생활화학제품(K-REACH)/화장품법 + KC for the appliance. The "6-second clears ANY smell" claim is physically unrealistic (be honest: deodorize+refresh, not de-smoke hair/wool in 6s).
**Monetization stack (the real money is B2B2C, per L14):** per-use fee (₩2-3k) · venue revenue-share · **fragrance-brand sampling/placement sponsorship** (brands pay to be the "finish" scent = a sampling channel, the scaling leg) · branded "scent of the month" passes. Avoid a data-moat claim (thin; watch L13).
**Improvements over the seed pitch:** (1) honest physics (60-90s, deodorize+refresh, not "any smell in 6s"); (2) the 포토이즘 placed-booth model as the proven template + placement-exclusivity as the moat; (3) the fragrance-sampling sponsorship leg as the actual revenue engine; (4) lead with a single high-density beachhead (one 회식 district) to test per-use WTP cheaply.
**Revive-trigger / cheap validation test:** put a single rented unit at one 고깃집/club-district exit for 2 weeks; if unprompted per-use volume clears the unit's daily payback AND a fragrance brand pays for the sampling slot → re-gate as MODE-CASH. Until then: PARK (do not build the fleet).
